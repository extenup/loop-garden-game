Loop Garden — privacy

Loop Garden by extenup stores your progress (rooms unlocked, stars, medals, hats, arena best score) and settings (sound, reduced motion) only in your browser's local storage on your device.

This web version has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no purchases, and it sends no personal data to the developer. Portal versions (for example on game websites) may show that portal's own ads and use that portal's own cloud save under the portal's privacy policy.

Clearing your browser's site data removes your progress. Contact: extenup@gmail.com
