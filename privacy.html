Loop Garden — privacy

Loop Garden by extenup stores your progress (rooms unlocked, stars, medals, hats, arena best score) and settings (sound, reduced motion) only in your browser's local storage on your device.

This web version has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no purchases, and it sends no personal data to the developer. Portal versions (for example on game websites such as Playgama) may show that portal's own ads, use that portal's own cloud save, and let that portal collect its own usage statistics (for example anonymous session and play-time data) under the portal's privacy policy. The developer adds no extra tracking to those versions.

Clearing your browser's site data removes your progress. Contact: extenup@gmail.com
